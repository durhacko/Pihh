## Preliminary shortlist

The top three are within 0.35 of each other, so this is effectively a tie at this confidence.

1. **C03 Patchwork.** **Pro:** the composition link is the most literal and explainable (f∘g ≠ g∘f, live). Zero hardware risk. Fast end-to-end slice. **Con:** risks reading as "photo filters"; technical depth is moderate unless typed ports or higher-order blocks land. **MVP:** ArUco cards → composed shader pipeline on a projector.
2. **C06 Thirds.** **Pro:** visually strong, judges become the subject, natural AI and hardware stretch. **Con:** close to commodity auto-framing; the "composition score" must be the star. **MVP:** webcam digital-crop virtual camera with score overlay.
3. **C01 Encore / C02 Fugue (tied).** **Pro:** high technical depth and a strong "wow". **Con:** Encore risks looking like "just particles" and needs visible decentralisation. Fugue's musical quality is subjective and the hall is noisy.

**Counterargument to the whole ranking:** no sponsor brief is known yet. If RS confirms the UNO Q, C03 and C06 gain. If a finance brief fits, C05 jumps. **Capture the briefs before the meeting** if at all possible.
